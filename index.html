Renovating means making big decisions without seeing clearly.

What is urgent, mandatory, fundable?

Rules, support and obligations are scattered across offices, simulators and contractors' websites.

What should it cost?

Without a price benchmark, there is no way to tell whether a quote is reasonable.

How do you compare quotes?

Every contractor prices in their own way. Quotes don't compare, and misunderstandings surface on site.

One journey, from idea to build.

Each step feeds the next. By the end, you know what you want, what it costs and who to trust with it.

  1. Define your project

    Your needs, your priorities, your home.

  2. Diagnose

    What your home needs, and the obligations that apply to you.

  3. Estimate the cost

    An honest range with visible assumptions, and what you can do yourself without losing support.

  4. Plan

    A roadmap, in the right order.

  5. Check permits

    What needs a permit, and what doesn't.

  6. Finance

    Loans, tax breaks and available support.

  7. Create your reno sheet

    A clear specification contractors can price.

  8. Choose and follow up

    Analysis of the quotes you receive, contractor checks, what to watch on site.
